06 — Paper

India @ AI & Deeptech Crossroads

Discussion Paper by Sanjay Phadke & Dr. Jaideep Prabhu

Download PDF Paper ↗
Part I — Executive Diagnosis

The $600B IT Buyback vs $20B Deeptech Gap

India generated ~$600 billion in IT services profits over the last decade, yet allocated only $10–20 billion to AI & deeptech. Bridging this gap requires connecting Cambridge's research rigour with Indian engineering depth and patient private capital.

Part II — The Three Frontier Tracks

Population Scale & Orbital Compute

Applying India's Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) model to sovereign AI compute; combining ISRO-grade spacecraft thermal engineering with UK space capital; and leveraging genomics to drive down healthcare cost curves globally.

Part III — The Bilateral Sangam

A Durable Confluence Ahead of UK–India CETA

Launched alongside the UK–India Free Trade Agreement, the network creates standing working pipelines across academia (Cambridge & IISER/IIT), founders, and institutional capital (A3 Capital, EPOCH, UK VC/Debt).
