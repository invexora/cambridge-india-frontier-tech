01: Thesis

India has a clear shot to be the world's capital of the AI app layer, estimated at $50 Trillion over the next decade.
The Gap

Success That Softened Us

~$600B Returned by IT services via buybacks vs $10–20B invested in deeptech

Prioritizing safe dividends over compounding capital into deeptech created a return-on-investment model looking through the rear-view mirror.

The Confluence

A Renaissance in Deeptech

$50 Trillion Global AI app & deeptech opportunity for UK–India founders

Founders building reusable rockets, orbital compute, quantum, and AI infrastructure. The network provides the bilateral glue between capital and research.

02: The Edge

Frugal is a different physics of cost.

ISRO landed Chandrayaan-3 on the lunar south pole for $75M, a fraction of conventional space missions. The same arithmetic governs the frontier AI application layer.
